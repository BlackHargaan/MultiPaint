Filament groups

Click a group to paint with it. The number is the filament slot it will be assigned to in the slicer.

Image projection

Renders your text as a decal in the active filament color. Position it over the model (drag / scroll / corner handle), then Apply decal below. Works on flat and gently curved faces.

Position the image over the model (drag it, corner handle or scroll to resize), orbit the model behind it, then Apply. Transparent PNG areas are skipped — perfect for logo decals.

Or the snapshot round-trip (for AI-editing an exact view):

Save a snapshot, paint on it in any editor (or ask an AI to recolor it) using your filament colors, then import it — the colors project back onto everything visible from that view. Don't crop or rotate the image; resizing is fine.
